# Lumora Labs Employee Handbook

*Fictional sample document created for the docsearch-ai-rag demo. Lumora Labs is not a real company.*

Version 3.2 — effective January 2026. This handbook applies to all full-time employees of Lumora Labs in every office. Contractors follow the terms of their individual agreements.

## 1. Working Hours and Flexibility

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you may organize your day as you see fit, as long as you work an average of 40 hours per week.

Lumora Labs follows a hybrid model. Employees assigned to an office are expected on site two days per week, chosen together with their team lead. Fully remote roles are marked as such in the offer letter.

Employees may work from another country for up to 30 calendar days per year. Longer stays require written approval from People Operations because of tax and immigration rules.

## 2. Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on March 31.

Vacation requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 business days in advance for absences longer than three days. Shorter absences need only 2 business days of notice.

Lumora Labs observes 11 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar for each office is published in the HR portal every December.

### Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

### Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave can be taken in up to two blocks within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## 3. Compensation and Benefits

Salaries are paid monthly on the last business day of the month. Salary bands are reviewed once per year in April, and adjustments take effect on May 1.

Every employee receives an annual learning budget of USD 1,500 for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Unused learning budget does not roll over. Requests above USD 500 need approval from your team lead.

The company provides private health insurance for employees and up to two dependents. Coverage starts on the first day of employment.

Employees working remotely receive a one-time home office stipend of USD 600 and a monthly internet allowance of USD 40.

## 4. Expenses and Travel

Business expenses must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days with an itemized receipt. Expenses submitted later than 60 days will not be reimbursed.

Economy class is the standard for flights under 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or longer, premium economy may be booked. The daily meal allowance during business travel is USD 75.

All international travel must be booked through the company travel portal so that we can reach you in an emergency.

## 5. Equipment

New employees receive a laptop on their first day. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT help desk within 24 hours.

## 6. Performance and Growth

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Each review includes a self-assessment, peer feedback from at least two colleagues, and a conversation with your team lead.

Internal transfers are encouraged. Employees become eligible to apply for an internal role after 12 months in their current position.

## 7. Code of Conduct

We expect everyone to act with integrity, respect and transparency. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.

Concerns can be raised with your team lead, with People Operations, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.

## 8. Leaving Lumora Labs

The notice period is 30 days for individual contributors and 60 days for managers. Company equipment must be returned on or before the last working day. Unused vacation days are paid out with the final salary.
